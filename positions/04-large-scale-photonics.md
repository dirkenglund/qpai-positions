# Large Scale Photonics

﻿Positions in Large-Scale Photonics for Cold-Atom Quantum Computing 
We are looking for experts in photonic integrated circuits (PICs) to join us in developing optical control systems for cold-atom based quantum computers. This project builds on our team’s expertise in large-scale PICs1–10 . Strong experience in PIC design and testing, as well as large-scale electronics control, will be needed. Additionally, in collaboration with our partners in the centre for ultracold atoms (CUA), we are currently constructing a cold atom tweezer array experiment for large scale neutral atom quantum computation powered by integrated photonics. This part of the project focuses on integration of  PIC technology with an atomic system and applications in quantum control.   
  

Project descriptions: 
1. Working with the Englund group at MIT, design world-leading large-scale PICs in collaboration11 with Sandia National Laboratory in Albuquerque, NM.
2. Construction of atomic tweezer array quantum computer and PIC integration at MIT
3. Put to use in collaboration with leading cold-atom atom teams at MIT and Harvard (groups of Vuletic, Greiner, Lukin)
4. Work in a fantastic team to perhaps discover the first instances of “quantum advantage”! 
5. There’s a possibility also for entrepreneurship & other forms of tech transition 
Contact: For more information, send an application email with CV to Prof. Dirk Englund of the MIT EECS Department at englund - at - mit.edu. Copy Dr. Adrian Menssen (amenssen - at - mit.edu) . Website: qplab.mit.edu.
