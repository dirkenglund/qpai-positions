# AI Augmented Scientific Discovery, focusing on Quantum Perception

## Overview

**Leads:** Kfir Sulimany kfir@mit.edu (Group Lead) and Prof. Dirk Englund englund@mit.edu (Principal Investigator)

The group develops learning systems that comprehend, regulate, and protect quantum and classical physical systems. Research spans artificial intelligence for science, quantum information processing, and machine learning on physical hardware, with emphasis on verification-hardware-aware methods.

## Core Research Questions

**Primary Question:** Can big data benefits be realized in the AI era while safeguarding individual privacy?

The team believes affirmative answers exist through "quantum‑secure multiparty machine‑learning protocols that preserve privacy while retaining functionality."

**Secondary Question:** What represents the minimal energy required to execute a computational task—quantum or classical—at finite temperature, time, and space constraints, and how closely can optical systems approach this theoretical limit?

## Project Descriptions

1. **AI for scientific discovery** – Physics-informed models and differentiable simulators for inverse design, experiment planning, experiment-in-the-loop optimization, and learning-based quantum control

2. **Machine learning on physical systems** – Computation leveraging optical and RF front-ends as learnable transforms for low-latency, low-energy inference

3. **Provably secure ML & quantum cryptography** – Protocols for privacy-preserving training/inference and quantum key distribution with information-leakage analyses

4. **Automated theorem proving** – Lean-based tools to verify algorithms, control stacks, and quantum-security proofs

## Experimental Platforms & Tools

- Integrated photonics (CMOS-compatible silicon photonics, lithium niobate)
- Nonlinear photonics (SPDC and parametric amplification)
- Quantum light & detection (single-photon sources and detectors)
- Analog/RF front-ends
- Software stack (differentiable simulators, experiment orchestrators, formal-verification hooks)
