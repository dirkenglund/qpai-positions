# Solid State Atoms for Quantum Information Processing

## Position Overview

The Quantum Photonics and AI Group at MIT seeks a postdoctoral researcher to work on solid-state atomic systems for quantum information processing. This position focuses on developing and characterizing color centers in diamond and other materials for quantum sensing, communication, and computing applications.

## Research Areas

- **Quantum sensing with solid-state spins** - Development of magnetometry and electrometry techniques using nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers
- **Quantum communication** - Integration of quantum memories with photonic networks
- **Cavity quantum electrodynamics** - Coupling solid-state emitters to optical cavities
- **Material science** - Growth and characterization of diamond and other host materials

## Experimental Focus

- Confocal microscopy and quantum control of individual color centers
- Development of hybrid quantum systems
- Integration with photonic integrated circuits
- Advanced fabrication techniques for quantum devices

## Qualifications

- PhD in Physics, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, or related field
- Experience with optical spectroscopy and/or quantum optics
- Background in solid-state physics or quantum information
- Strong programming skills (Python, MATLAB, etc.)
