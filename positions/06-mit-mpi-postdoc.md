# MIT-MPI Postdoc

﻿Joint MIT-MPI Postdoctoral Fellow Positions
The laboratories of Prof. Dirk Englund (MIT) and Prof. Joyce Poon (Max Planck Institute of Microstructure Physics [MPI-MSP]) have positions for jointly supervised Postdoctoral Fellows in the field of integrated photonics. The positions may be taken up in MIT in Cambridge, MA, USA or MPI-MSP in Halle (Saale), Germany.


Project 1: Photonic Neural Network Accelerators for Scalable Brain-Machine Interfaces    
Large-scale photonic circuits have opened new research directions in machine learning and neurotechnology [1–11]. To overcome the inference bottleneck from implantable neural probes (left: MPI-MSP neural probe), we are seeking a postdoctoral fellow to develop real-time signal and imaging processing of neural recordings based on analog machine learning accelerators (right: MIT photonic deep neural network inference processor trained by in-situ learning [5]).

Project 3: UV-VIS Integrated Photonics 
  

In collaboration with foundry partners, our teams have been developing integrated photonics platforms for visible (VIS) and ultraviolet (UV) wavelength ranges [12–16]. These technologies enable highly scalable photonic integrated circuits for large-scale machine learning and quantum control. We are seeking a postdoctoral fellow to develop UV-VIS range photonic integrated circuits and components for these applications. 


Position Details and How to Apply 
Your tasks
* Investigate and develop photonic integrated circuits for neural recording processing or quantum information
* Design, layout, and measure integrated photonic devices and circuits
* Integrate the photonic chips into microsystems that include, for example, electronics and discrete optical components
* Train and work closely with other students and researchers
* Interact with collaborators and external partners when necessary
Your Profile
* You have earned or will soon earn a Ph.D. Degree in electrical engineering, physics, or a related discipline
* You are experienced in the simulation, design, and layout of integrated photonic devices and circuits
* You are proficient with CAD and simulation tools such as Lumerical, COMSOL
* You are experienced in the characterization of integrated photonic devices and circuits (experience in design-fab-test cycles using photonic foundries is useful)
* You have excellent written and oral communication skills in English
Your application
Please upload your CV and the names of two references as a single pdf file to https://s.gwdg.de/H0AT77. If you have a project preference, please indicate in a cover letter. Please name your file with your name as lastname_firstname.pdf. Your file has been successfully uploaded if you see your filename under “Uploaded files” after your submission.
If you have questions, please contact Prof. Dirk Englund (englund@mit.edu and admin balzer@mit.edu ) or Prof. Joyce Poon (joyce.poon@mpi-halle.mpg.de).
